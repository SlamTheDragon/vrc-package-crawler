2. **What the Crawler Indexes**
    1. **Factual Metadata Only.** The crawler collects and indexes only public, objective, factual data from publicly accessible storefront pages. Indexed facts include: package names, reverse-DNS identifiers, semantic version numbers, platform compatibility tags, pricing, and canonical storefront URLs.
    2. **Copyright Basis for Factual Data.** In accordance with 17 U.S.C. 102(b) and *Feist Publications, Inc. v. Rural Telephone Service Co.* (499 U.S. 340), raw factual specifications such as version numbers, identifiers, and pricing do not carry copyright protection. Creative marketing descriptions and lore are truncated to brief snippets to avoid copyright appropriation.
    3. **Three-State Timestamp Confidence Rubric.** The Project records asset creation timestamps under a strict three-state confidence rubric:
        * `confirmed`: Directly extracted from authoritative upstream platform metadata.
        * `inferred`: Derived from the earliest verified commit or changelog entry.
        * `unknown`: When no upstream publication date exists, the timestamp is recorded as `NULL`. The pipeline does not substitute local crawl timestamps for missing upstream publication dates.
    4. **Public Platforms Indexed.** The Project indexes publicly accessible listings from: BOOTH.pm, Gumroad, Jinxxy, itch.io, GitHub, and community registries such as VRCArena (subject to Section 5).

3. **What the Crawler Never Indexes**
    1. **Zero-Binary Invariant.** The Software does not download, cache, store, unpack, mirror, or redistribute proprietary compiled assets, textures, or binary code. Prohibited formats include `.unitypackage`, `.vpmz`, `.zip`, `.tar.gz`, `.rar`, `.7z`, `.fbx`, `.obj`, `.blend`, `.prefab`, `.asset`, `.anim`, textures, audio, shaders, and executables (`.dll`, `.exe`, `.so`). The crawler does not use platform management APIs to extract catalog data.     4. **No Credential Use.** The crawler operates strictly as an unauthenticated, logged-out public guest. It contains no credential scrapers, no session token handlers, and bypasses no password gates or access controls.
